How signing up works

  1. Sign in below, either with Google or with a code we email you. Any email address works.
  2. Tell us who you are. Use the same email the fellowship knows you by.
  3. An admin approves you. If an admin has already added you, you’re all set straight away.
Want “Sign in with Google” with a USC, Keck or other non-Gmail address?

You can make a Google account that uses your current email, without getting Gmail:

  1. Open accounts.google.com/signup. If it asks, choose Create account → For my personal use.
  2. Enter your name and the other details it asks for.
  3. When it offers a Gmail address, choose Use your existing email and type your current email.
  4. Enter the code Google emails you, then choose a password and finish.
  5. Come back here and choose Sign in with Google with that account.

You don’t need a Google account to use the sites (the emailed code works too). You do need one to see the fellowship calendars in Google Calendar.
